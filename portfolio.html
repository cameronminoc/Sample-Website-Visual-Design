About

I run a small independent studio focused on identity and editorial work for clients who care about material, pacing, and restraint. Most projects start with a typeface and a question, rather than a mood board.

Before going independent in 2021, I spent five years as a senior designer at a branding studio in Porto, working on hospitality and cultural identities across Southern Europe.
